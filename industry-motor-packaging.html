Heavy-duty EPS packaging for motors and electrical equipment

A motor is heavy, oddly shaped, and has a shaft that will punch through weak packaging. We mould base and top sets in a higher-density EPS, with the load carried on the motor frame and feet, a recess for the shaft and key, and clearance for the terminal box and eye bolt.

Common questions

Can the same set serve several frame sizes?

Often two or three adjacent frame sizes can share a set with a different insert; larger jumps need their own mould. Send the frame list and we will group them.

What about rust protection?

EPS does not absorb water, but it is not a vapour barrier. For export, combine the insert with a VCI bag or film; we can size the cavity to allow for it.

Do you make the pallet-level packaging too?

We make the EPS components, including layer pads and corner buffers. Pallets and strapping are usually arranged by your dispatch team.

Send a drawing or photos with dimensions, or courier the item to the factory. We design the pack and send a sample for approval before production.
