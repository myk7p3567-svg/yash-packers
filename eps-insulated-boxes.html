Common questions

How long will a box keep contents cold?

It depends on wall thickness, how much ice or gel pack is used, ambient temperature and how often the box is opened. We can advise a wall thickness and coolant quantity for your route.

Are the boxes food safe?

EPS is widely used for food and seafood packaging. Tell us the product and we will confirm the grade and any secondary liner you may need.

Can I print or brand the box?

Labels and stickers are the usual approach. Ask about options when you request a quote.
