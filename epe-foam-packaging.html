EPE foam packaging

Expanded polyethylene (EPE) is a closed-cell foam that is softer and more flexible than EPS. It does not crumble, it is non-abrasive, and it recovers after compression, which makes it the right choice for polished surfaces, repeat-use packaging and products with delicate finishes.

Common questions

EPS or EPE: which should I use?

EPS is rigid, cheaper and better for heavy loads and insulation. EPE is soft, flexible and kinder to finished surfaces. Many packs use both: an EPS outer for structure and EPE where it touches the product. See the guide in Resources.

Is EPE reusable?

Yes. EPE recovers its shape after compression, so it suits returnable trays and packaging that is opened and closed many times.

Can the foam be laminated or coloured?

Yes, within the options available at the time of order. Ask when requesting a quote.
