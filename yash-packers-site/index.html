How a custom pack is made

Most enquiries are for a product that has never been packed in EPS before. This is the route from enquiry to production.

See manufacturing capabilities

  1. Share the product

    A drawing, 3D file, photos with dimensions, or the physical item couriered to us.

  2. Design the cavity

    We decide wall thickness, density and split lines so the pack protects the product and fits your carton.

  3. Make the mould

    A production mould is manufactured for the design. Mould cost is quoted up front and is a one-time charge.

  4. Approve a sample

    You test the fit and, if needed, a drop test. Changes are made before any volume is produced.

  5. Produce and deliver

    Production runs to your schedule, with delivery across Delhi NCR and by transport across India.
