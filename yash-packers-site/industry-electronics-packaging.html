EPS and EPE packaging for electronics and appliances

Electronics fail in transit for three reasons: the product moves inside the carton, the insert presses on a display or connector, or the pack does not handle a corner drop. We design the cavity so the product is held on its housing, clear of screens, ports and cables, with crush zones where the carton takes the impact.

Common questions

Will the insert mark a glossy surface?

EPS can scuff a high-gloss or soft-touch finish over a long journey. Where the pack touches such a surface we add an EPE pad or line the cavity with EPE.

Can one insert hold the unit and all accessories?

Yes. Separate pockets for the remote, adapter, cables and manuals are moulded into the same piece, which also stops accessories rattling against the unit.

How do we handle multiple models with similar housings?

A single mould can sometimes serve several models with small interchangeable inserts. Send the range and we will check.

Start with the product

Send a drawing or photos with dimensions, or courier the item to the factory. We design the pack and send a sample for approval before production.
