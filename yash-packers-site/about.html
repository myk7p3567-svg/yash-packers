What happens on the floor

  1. Pre-expansion

    Raw EPS beads are expanded with steam to the target density. Density is set here, so it is checked for every job.

  2. Moulding

    Expanded beads are fused in a shape mould or block mould under steam and pressure.

  3. Cutting and finishing

    Blocks are cut into sheets, pipe sections and profiles. Moulded parts are trimmed and inspected.

  4. EPE conversion

    EPE sheet is die-cut, routed, laminated and glued into boxes, inserts and fitments.

  5. Inspection and dispatch

    Dimensions, fit and finish are checked against the approved sample before packing for dispatch.

Quality

Every new design is approved on a physical sample before a production run. Density is controlled at pre-expansion and checked on finished parts. Dimensional checks are made against the approved sample, and drop or compression tests can be arranged for critical products.
