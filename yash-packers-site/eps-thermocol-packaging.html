EPS thermocol packaging

Expanded polystyrene (EPS, commonly called thermocol) is light, rigid and absorbs impact, which makes it the standard material for protecting industrial and consumer products in transit. Yash Packers moulds EPS packaging to the exact shape of your product, so it cannot move inside the carton and arrives in the condition it left your line.

Common questions

Can you make packaging for a product you have never seen?

Yes. Send a drawing, a 3D file or the physical product. The engineering team designs the cavity, produces a sample for fit approval, and only then starts the production run.

What is the difference between EPS and thermocol?

None. Thermocol is the common Indian trade name for expanded polystyrene (EPS). Both words refer to the same material.

How do I choose density?

Heavier or more fragile products need a higher density so the foam does not compress permanently on impact. Tell us the product weight and how it will be shipped and we will recommend a grade.

Do you supply the outer carton as well?

We supply the EPS packaging. We are happy to work with your carton supplier so the insert and carton are sized together, or to recommend one.
