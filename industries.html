Industries we pack for

The material is the same; the engineering is different for every product and route. Below is what we typically make for each sector. If yours is not listed, the process is the same: send the product and we design around it.

Automotive components

Headlamps, mirrors, bumpers and trims arrive with scratches and cracks when they are packed in loose material. A moulded EPS or EPE cavity holds each part on its strongest surfaces and keeps finished faces clear of contact.

Typical products: EPS inserts for lamps and clusters, EPE fitments for painted parts, returnable EPE trays for line-side delivery, corner buffers for bulk cartons.

Request a quote for automotive components

Electronics and appliances

Precision cavities for set-top boxes, inverters, meters, panels, printers and small appliances, with space for cables and accessories.

Read the electronics and appliances page

Motors and electrical equipment

Heavy-duty base and top sets for electric motors, pumps, alternators and switchgear, including shaft and terminal recesses.

Read the motors and electrical equipment page

Pharma and cold chain

Vaccines, reagents and samples need a known holding time between dispatch and delivery. Wall thickness and coolant quantity are matched to the route and ambient conditions.

Typical products: insulated vaccine boxes with lids, sample carriers, gel-pack recesses, secondary cartons sized to the box.

Request a quote for pharma and cold chain

Glassware, ceramics and consumer goods

Mugs, bottles, lamps, cookware and gift sets break when pieces touch each other. Nested cavities separate every item.

Typical products: multi-cavity EPS trays, two-part gift boxes, EPE inserts for premium finishes.

Request a quote for glassware, ceramics and consumer goods

Food and seafood

Seafood, dairy, meat and ice cream need insulation and, sometimes, drainage and stacking.

Typical products: 10 litre ice boxes, fish boxes with stacking lids, custom shippers for chilled delivery.

Request a quote for food and seafood

Construction and HVAC

Insulation and void forming where a light, rigid, moisture-resistant material is needed.

Typical products: 30 mm and 50 mm insulation sheets, half-round pipe sections cut to pipe diameter, void formers and spacers.

Request a quote for construction and hvac
